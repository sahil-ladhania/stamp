## 2. The four changes

**A. Eligibility gate** (`extraction.ts`, `prompts/extract-sections.ts`, `verify/verify-gates.ts`, `scoring/gate-facts.ts`, `guide/assemble-sections.ts`)

- The gates output gains `eligibility`: a list of `{kind, claim}` with kind `organizations_only`, `user_threshold` or `partner_application`. The model quotes; it sets no label.
- Code checks each one, in the same order as the other gate facts: the quote is on an official page (`keepOfficial`); it holds the words of its kind (an organisation or company word with an apply, access or production word; a number with a users or MAU unit; a partner word with a program or apply word); and the support check, extended to these items, may remove one. An administrator approving apps for their own workspace is not eligibility (the same rule as for review gates).
- What survives becomes (1) a requirement claim, appended after the model's requirements, Sourced on the quote; (2) the review gate `partner_vetting` with that quote as evidence, **only when the gate is unknown or weaker**; a stronger verified gate stays; (3) a blocker whose symptom is that claim (cause, fix and contact are the usual unknown claims), so it can be ranked (C).
- No verified quote means no fact: nothing is added, and the gate stays what it was (often unknown, "Not rated"). An unquoted statement is never shown.

**B. Test mode** (same files, plus `extract/duration-text.ts`, `web/src/utils/extract-duration.ts`)

- The gates output gains `testMode` (`available`, `unavailable`, `unknown`) with its claim, and `testUserCap` (a whole number that must appear in that quote). The prompt keeps "paid plan needed to test" as a prerequisite, as now.
- Code accepts `available` only from an official quote with a testing word (development mode, sandbox, test account, allowlist) and no approval word that makes it conditional. Then the first-test **wait** claim is "No wait", Sourced on that quote, and the cap becomes a claim under Limits.
- Not changed: first-test **effort** stays a stated time or Unknown; "No wait" is the only non-numeric value, written by code.

**C. Top blocker, in code** (`guide/top-blocker.ts`)

- Order: a blocker with a non-forum Sourced symptom beats a forum-sourced or Uncertain one; then eligibility (4) over approval (3) over setup (2) over limit (1); then the existing evidence tie-break. A blocker made from an eligibility fact is marked by the code that made it, not guessed from its words.
- A forum-sourced blocker can still be top when it is all there is. Its source chip says forum.

**D. Enterprise and the archetype words**

- `enterprisePlanEvidence` goes through `keepOfficial`, a plan-word check (`enterprise`, `plan`, `tier`, `subscription`, `paid`, `premium`) and the support check. An organisation quote no longer sets `needsEnterprisePlan`.
- Web blurbs (Stamp's own categories, not provider facts): `partner_gated`: "Production access is by application, for approved partners or eligible organizations."; `enterprise`: "Needs a special or enterprise plan, as the docs state." Both blurbs and their tests change.
